fix(dig): only a server-confirmed break counts

mineflayer marks a dug block as air locally the moment bot.dig() resolves
(_updateBlockState), so the previous check ("blockAt is not the block anymore")
was always true and behaviours counted phantom chops as progress - the bot
reported 3/3 logs on an untouched tree.

A dig now counts only when the server confirms it (block_change at that
position) or a drop appears next to it. Otherwise the dig is retried and then
reported as dig_rejected with the distance, so the loop can avoid that target.

Verified against the live 26.3 server: digging the block under the bot breaks it
(broke grass_block, the bot falls one block), while phantom targets are now
reported instead of counted.
